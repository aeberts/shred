# Readiness evidence

Judge the bounded outcome, observable criteria, and unresolved material product decisions separately from the exact selected issue and cited sources. Missing, malformed, contradictory, stale, or unavailable evidence is incomplete; it cannot authorize Ready. Keep atomic yes/no answers and source versions. Do not roll up a probability into authority or substitute readiness for independent QA. The Coordinator resolves disagreements and preserves the human's material intent questions. Blockers, repository identity, and live authority require separate deterministic checks.
