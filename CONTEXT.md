# Guitar Practice

Shred is a guitar practice tracker for its owner's personal practice.

## Language

**Practice Goal**:
A specific guitar ability the player wants to integrate into their playing, with an explicit success criterion. A goal can cover varied test prompts, such as locating any requested note on the fretboard.
_Avoid_: Priority, aspiration

**Exercise**:
A repeatable practice activity intended to help the player reach one Practice Goal.

**Exercise Sequence**:
An ordered series of Exercises that support one Practice Goal.

**Next Time Note**:
A player-written reminder of where to resume within an Exercise or Song Section.

**Instructions**:
The essential directions and material that define what the player practises in an Exercise or Song Section.

**Explanation**:
Player-supplied guidance that explains the concept or method behind practice material.

**Hint**:
Player-supplied help intended to support practice until the player can perform without it.

**Success Criterion**:
An explicit standard the player uses to assess an Exercise or judge whether a Practice Goal has been reached. It can be a measured threshold or a qualitative assessment; an Exercise's criterion can be narrower than its goal's criterion.
_Avoid_: Success, completion

**Learned**:
The player has confirmed that they understand the material in an Exercise and can use it deliberately. Learning is assessed per Exercise and is an intermediate checkpoint before integration of the Practice Goal.

**Learning Practice**:
Practice directed toward understanding and deliberately using the material in an Exercise.

**Mastery Practice**:
Practice directed toward integrating an Exercise's material into the player's broader musical ability.

**Integrated**:
The player can use the ability described by a Practice Goal reliably in their playing. The player judges this from repeated evidence against the Success Criterion.

**Concept**:
A broad musical idea or guitar skill that can support specific Practice Goals. Examples include using a diminished scale over the IV chord and fretboard knowledge.

**Practice Session**:
A period of guitar practice organized into learning, mastery, and repertoire practice blocks.

**Session Plan**:
The selected practice blocks, planned durations, and practice material for a Practice Session.

**Practice Block**:
A portion of a Practice Session dedicated to one practice purpose.

**Practice Day**:
A distinct local calendar day on which the player confirms they practised a particular Exercise. Each Exercise has its own sequence of Practice Days.

**Practice Confirmation**:
The player's explicit statement that they practised an Exercise. It establishes a Practice Day without requiring a performance assessment.

**Repertoire**:
The collection of songs the player is learning or maintaining for complete performance from memory.

**Song**:
A complete musical piece the player wants to perform from memory from start to finish.

**Song Section**:
A player-named portion of one Song used as a unit of learning toward a complete performance. It can be any size useful for practice.

**Song Order**:
The ordered occurrences of Song Sections that make up a Song. The same Song Section can occur more than once.

**Practice Range**:
Consecutive Song Section occurrences practised together to connect sections toward a complete performance.

**Learned Song Section**:
A Song Section the player has explicitly confirmed they can play from memory. It may still need polish.

**Learned Song**:
A Song the player has explicitly confirmed they can play in full from memory. It may still need polish and remains eligible for repertoire review.

**Song Review**:
An attempt to play a previously learned Song from memory for the player to assess.

**Review Interval**:
The number of calendar days from a Song Review's assessed performance date to that Song's next scheduled review. For a continued review, the performance date is when the assessed performance finished.

**Due Song**:
A Learned Song whose scheduled review date has arrived or passed.

**Needs Work**:
A Song Review assessment in which memory gaps or mistakes prevent a satisfactory full performance.

**Playable**:
A Song Review assessment in which the player finishes from memory but the performance needs polish.

**Performance-ready**:
A Song Review assessment in which the player plays through confidently, with little or no mistakes. It describes the assessed performance rather than a permanent property of the Song.

**Result**:
Evidence the player records from deliberately assessing performance against a Success Criterion, linked to a Practice Goal and optionally an Exercise. It contains a met, partly met, or not met assessment, optional player-defined measurements, and optional notes, and retains the criterion and relevant material assessed.

**Measurement**:
A player-entered value for an aspect of an assessed performance, such as positions found or seconds taken. The player defines what is measured and its unit; a Measurement supports a Result without determining its assessment automatically.

**Test Context**:
Player-recorded details of the conditions used for an assessment, such as the requested note or whether guidance was visible. Context fields are defined with the Success Criterion and reused across Results.

**Unfinished Song Review**:
A Song Review attempt that the player has left before recording a performance assessment. It can be continued later and does not change the Song's rating or review schedule.
